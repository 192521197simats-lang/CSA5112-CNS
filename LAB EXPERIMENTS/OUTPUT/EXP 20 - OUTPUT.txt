CBC ciphertext error propagation:
A bit error in C1 corrupts all of P1 (block cipher output) and flips the matching bit in P2.
Blocks P3 onward are not affected by a single C1 bit error.
A source plaintext bit error in P1 changes C1; subsequent CBC ciphertext blocks generally change.
At the receiver, P1 is corrupted, and later plaintext blocks are also affected through chaining.
